Your Xbox 360 collection, dashboard style

Keep track of every game you own and every one you're after, in an app styled after the console's dashboard. It runs at home and opens in any browser on your network.

Every copy, every detail

Open a game to see what IGDB knows about it, and note what only you know about your copy.

  • Editions, SteelBooks, region, condition and what you paid
  • Who borrowed which game, and since when
  • Play status, your own rating and a few words of review
  • Critic score, time to beat, series and studio

Check your shelf from the shop

The same library on your PC, tablet and phone. Look up what you own before you buy a game twice, then add it with one tap. Over a VPN it works away from home too.

  • Runs on a PC, Mac, NAS or Raspberry Pi
  • One folder holds it all, with a backup every day
  • An optional login for the rest of your household

Everything a shelf needs

Search and import

Find a game on IGDB and its cover and details come along, or paste a whole list at once.

A wishlist

What to buy next, how badly you want it, and which orders are on their way.

Cases, covers or a list

Browse 3D cases, a wall of covers or a compact list, at any size.

Tags and filters

Your own tags work as collections; filter by status, year, format and more.

Your data, your files

Up and running in three steps

All it needs is Docker on a computer that stays on at home: a Windows PC, a Mac, a Linux box, a Synology, QNAP or Unraid NAS, or a Raspberry Pi 4. No programming, nothing to build.

  1. 1

    Make a folder

    Install Docker if you don't have it yet. Make a folder called xenonshelf and save this in it as docker-compose.yml: 

    services:
  xenonshelf:
    image: ghcr.io/k4rm4work/xenonshelf:latest
    container_name: xenonshelf
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - '8360:3000'
    volumes:
      - ./library-data:/data
  2. 2

    Start it

    Open a terminal in that folder and run this. On a NAS, paste the file into a new project or stack in its Docker app instead. 

    docker compose up -d
  3. 3

    Open it

    Go to http://<your computer's address>:8360 in any browser on your network. A short guide helps you add the free IGDB keys for covers and search, and an optional login.

Updating, backups and more options are in the README.
