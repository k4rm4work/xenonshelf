XenonShelfTM

Your Xbox 360 collection and wishlist, in the style of the console's dashboard.

XenonShelf runs on a computer or NAS in your home and opens in any browser on your network. Free, open source, and your data never leaves your house.

What it does

Your whole shelf

Search for a game and its cover and details come from IGDB, or import a whole list at once.

More than titles

Mark what you have played and note editions, SteelBooks and games you lent out.

A wishlist

Keep track of what to buy next, next to what you already own.

Any device at home

Runs on a PC, Mac or NAS and opens in the browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

Your data stays yours

One folder on your own machine, daily backups, and export to a spreadsheet any time.

Six languages

English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch and Polski.

Install in three steps

  1. 1

    Make a folder

    You need Docker on a computer that stays on at home. Make a folder called xenonshelf and save this in it as docker-compose.yml: 

    services:
  xenonshelf:
    image: ghcr.io/k4rm4work/xenonshelf:latest
    container_name: xenonshelf
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - '8360:3000'
    volumes:
      - ./library-data:/data
  2. 2

    Start it

    Open a terminal in that folder and run this. On a NAS, paste the file into a new project or stack in its Docker app instead. 

    docker compose up -d
  3. 3

    Open it

    Go to http://<your computer's address>:8360 in any browser on your network. A short guide helps you add the free IGDB keys for covers and search, and an optional login.

Updating, backups and more options are in the README.
